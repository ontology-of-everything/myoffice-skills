来源：Patrick Winston, "How to Speak", MIT OpenCourseWare IAP 2018。
完整原文 1 小时；视频 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Unzc731iCUY

## 0. 底层公理：One Language Processor

> "We humans only have one language processor and if your language processor is engaged browsing the web or reading your email, you're distracted and worse yet you distract all the people around you."

**写作含义**：读者的注意力是稀缺资源。任何"在同一处既要读又要听"的设计都会失败。
- 一张塞满字的幻灯片 → 观众读字，没听你讲
- 一段长文里塞高密度术语 → 读者卡住，连带上下文都丢

## 1. 起：Empowerment Promise

### 原则

> "Start with empowerment promise. Tell people what they're going to know at the end of the hour that they didn't know at the beginning of the hour. It's the reason for being here."

> "Some people think the right thing to do is to start a talk with a joke. I don't recommend it. In the beginning of a talk, people are still putting their laptops away, becoming adjusted to your speaking parameters, your vocal parameters. They're not ready for a joke. So it doesn't work very well — they usually fall flat."

### 模板

```
在 [时长] 内，你会 [得到 X]，其中至少 [N] 点会让你 [具体应用场景]。
```

**示例（技术博客）**：
> 在这篇 10 分钟的阅读里，你会拿到 4 条我用了 7 年打磨出来的写作启发式，其中第 2 条能让你明天的专栏开头就不一样。

**示例（视频脚本）**：
> 看到结尾，你会知道为什么 Patrick Winston 把这堂课叫"How to Speak"而不是"Public Speaking 101"——而且这套启发式可以套到下一篇你写的稿子里。

### Passion 同步

> "I asked incoming freshmen and senior faculty how they've been inspired. The freshmen were inspired by a high school teacher who told them they could do it. The senior faculty were inspired by someone who helped them see a problem in a new way. But everyone was inspired by someone who exhibited passion about what they were doing."

→ 文章 / 视频里**讲你 care 的理由**，不要伪装。

## 2. 承：四个常驻启发式（4 Sample Heuristics）

### 2.1 Cycling 循环

> "Cycle on the subject — go around it, go around it again. Tell them what you want to tell them, tell them again, and then tell them a third time. Some people say it as if people weren't intelligent. The reason is: at any given moment about 20% of you will be fogged out, no matter what the lecture is."

**写作落地**：
- 关键结论 3 次出现：开篇预告 → 正文展开 → 结尾回顾
- 不要简单复读。每次循环换一个切入角度（例子 / 反例 / 应用）

**示例结构（公众号长文）**：
1. 开篇：「今天讲 3 条」→ 列出
2. 第一节讲完 → 「还记得开头那条吗？现在看个反例」
3. 结尾 → 「回去看那 3 条，第 2 条其实是……」

### 2.2 Fence 围栏

> "Build a fence around my idea so that it's not confused with somebody else's idea. 'My algorithm might seem similar to Jones's, except his is exponential and mine is linear.' That's putting a fence around your idea."

**写作落地**：
- 显式与已有方案 / 常见误解对比
- 用一个对照表格、一句"vs."句式

**句式模板**：
- "和 X 不同，我的方法 [差异点]，因为 [理由]。"
- "这不是 [常见误解]，而是 [你的定义]。"
- "X 是 A；我的是 B。"

### 2.3 Verbal Punctuation 言语标点

> "Because people will occasionally fog out and need to get back on the bus, you need to provide some landmark places. So I might say 'the first thing we're going to do is... then we're going to... and among these I've talked about the first idea that's cycling, the second idea building a fence, and now the third idea is verbal punctuation.' I'm enumerating, I'm providing numbers, I'm giving you a sense that there's a seam in the talk."

**写作落地**：
- 用序号：「**第 N 点**」「最后一个原则」「中间插一句」
- 段落开头放锚句（anchor sentence），让扫读者能跳读
- 章节末尾预告下一节：「下一节我们会处理……」

### 2.4 Question 提问 + 7 秒

> "How much dead air can there be? How long can I pause? I counted 7 seconds — it seems like an eternity to me to wait and not say anything for 7 seconds, but that's the standard amount of time you can wait for an answer."

> "The question has to be carefully chosen. It can't be too obvious because then people will be embarrassed to say what the answer is. Can't be too hard because nobody will have anything to say."

**写作落地**：
- 每个大节后**留一个让读者自问自答的小问题**
- 在文末给答案（让读者有"对答案"的满足感）

**视频 / 演讲落地**：
- 抛问题 → **沉默 ≥7 秒**（"你们不会真死的"）
- 问题不能太显然（听众不好意思答），也不能太难（没人能答）

## 3. Time and Place（环境要素）

| 维度 | 规则 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| 时间 | 11am — 人们已醒，未用餐后，未疲劳 | Winston 演讲开头 |
| 灯光 | **保持全亮** — 暗灯 = 大脑认为该睡觉 | "It's extremely hard to see slides through closed eyelids." |
| 踩点 | **提前到**演讲场地试一遍（"cased"） | 来自「银行抢劫踩点」类比 |
| 上座率 | 至少**半满** — 全场 10 人反而尴尬 | "If there are 10 people, everyone would be wondering what's going on" |

**写作落地**（线上场景）：
- 发布时间：用户**活跃时段**（不是深夜、不是午餐）
- 阅读环境：手机 / 桌面？段落长度要适配
- 标题 / 头图：让"扫过信息流"的人也能被钩住

## 4. Tools：Boards / Props / Slides

### 根本：Empathic Mirroring 镜像神经元

> "When you're sitting up there watching me write on the board, all those little mirror neurons in your head become actuated and you can feel yourself writing on the blackboard. You can't do that with a slide. You can't do it with a picture. You need to see it in the physical world."

**应用**：能用 prop（实物）的场景，永远优于图。
- 演示算法 → 一张纸 + 折角
- 演示数据流向 → 一根绳子 + 三个球
- 演示化学反应 → 真做一遍（或录视频）

### Boards vs Slides 选择

| 目的 | 工具 | 理由 |
|---|---|---|
| Teach / Inform | 黑板 / 手写 | 镜像；节奏 ≈ 听众吸收速度 |
| Persuade / Expose | 幻灯片 | 控制节奏；远程 / 多人观看 |

### 黑板的三个 virtues

> "Many novices find themselves suddenly aware of their hands, as if their hands were private parts that shouldn't be exposed. They go into their pockets. This is considered insulting in some parts of the world."

1. **Graphic quality** — 实时画图能力
2. **Speed property** — 书写速度 ≈ 吸收速度
3. **Target** — 板书给手有事做，让眼睛留在讲者身上

**Seymour Papert 故事**：他一直在指板书，**但所指的内容其实与他在讲的话无关**——指的动作本身让观众感到"被引导"。

### Prop 的两个范例

1. **壁炉 + 手稿**（Ibsen《海达·高布乐》）：壁炉火焰从 embers → all-consuming，观众预感到手稿会被烧 — **戏剧张力 = 时间维度上的 prop**
2. **自行车轮 + 胶带**（Papert）：用胶带标一小段，吹动它 → 教"right-hand screw rule"。**思维方式的转变**可以用物理动作演示

## 5. 幻灯片七戒（Slide Crimes）

> "The crimes occur. They actually occur."

1. **Read your transparencies** — 听众识字，朗读冒犯
2. **Too many slides** — 删
3. **Too many words per slide** — 删
4. **Background junk** — 删
5. **Logos** — 删
6. **Title** — 删（口头讲就行）
7. **Laser pointer** — 不用，会让你转身丢眼神交流；改用箭头 + 文字："now look at guy number 1"

**字号自检**：打印一张 40pt 的 sample slide，从最后一排能否看清？能 → OK。

### "Too heavy" 自检

> "When people ask me to review a presentation, I ask them to print it out and lay it out on a table. When they do that, it's easy to see whether the talk is too heavy — too much text, not enough air, not enough white space, not enough imagery."

**写作落地**（视频脚本）：
- 打印 / 大纲视图：一眼能看出哪页"太重"
- 重 → 拆成两页 + 加一张图

### Epex Lan（密集页的特例）

> "You can get away with exactly one of these in your presentation — one per talk, one per paper, one per book. That's what epex lan is."

→ 一篇里**只允许一张「故意密集」的页**（用来震撼或承重）。

## 6. Informing（告知型）

### 三类 Inspiration

| 对象 | 什么能激励他 |
|---|---|
| Freshman | 有人告诉他们 "you can do it" |
| Senior faculty | 帮他们**用新方式看老问题** |
| All | **Passion** — 展现你对所讲之事在乎 |

### 教学 = 教故事

> "We are storytelling animals. We develop story understanding and manipulation skills with fairy tales in childhood and continue through professional schools. If that's what thinking is all about, then to teach people how to think you provide them with: stories, questions about those stories, mechanisms for analyzing stories, ways of putting stories together, ways of evaluating how reliable the story is."

→ 任何"教"的载体（书 / 课 / 长文）应包含 5 件套：
1. **Stories** — 故事
2. **Questions** — 关于故事的提问
3. **Mechanisms** — 分析机制
4. **Composition** — 组装方法
5. **Evaluation** — 评估方法（信源可靠度）

## 7. Persuading（说服型）

### 7.1 Oral Exams 答辩

> "The most usual reason for failing an oral exam is failure to situate and failure to practice."

- **Situate**：把研究放进上下文（"this problem is being pursued all over the world, no progress in 30 years..."）
- **Practice**：彩排

> "If people know what you're doing, they will hallucinate that there's material in your presentation that isn't there. Your supervisor is not a good person to debug a talk — they know what you're doing. Get friends who don't know your work. Say: 'If you can't make me cry, I won't value you as a friend anymore.'"

**额外的牌**：
> "The amount of flack you get is proportional to age. The older somebody is, the more they understand where they are in the world. Young people are trying to show old people how smart they are — so be so vicious."

→ 答辩委员会**老委员越多越好**。

### 7.2 Job Talks（求职 talk / 工作汇报）

> "I asked two senior faculty: what do you look for in a candidate? Within a microsecond: 'They have to show us vision' and 'They have to show us they've done something.' How long? — 5 minutes."

→ **5 分钟内必须亮出**：
- **Vision**：problem someone cares about + new approach
- **Done something**：list the steps taken to realize the vision

**通用求职 / 工作演讲框架**：

```
[Promise]    今天 20 分钟讲我对 X 的看法
[Problem]    这个 problem 为什么有人 care
[Approach]   我的新切入是什么
[Steps]      我做了哪些步骤（即使没做完也能列出）
[Body]       blah blah blah
[Contributions] 镜像开头的 problem，列出 actual contributions
```

### 7.3 Getting Famous（让作品被记住）— Winston Star (5S)

> "I call this Winston Star. Every one of the items starts with S."

Winston 自己的 arch learning 工作"accidentally had all the elements"：

| S | Element | 例子（arch learning） |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Symbol** 象征 | 拱门（arch）本身 |
| 2 | **Slogan** 口号 | "one-shot learning" |
| 3 | **Surprise** 惊喜 | 用 1 个例子就能学会，不必百万 |
| 4 | **Salient idea** 突出概念 | "near miss"（近失） |
| 5 | **Story** 故事 | 完整叙事：怎么做的 / 为什么重要 |

**应用检查**：你正在写 / 讲的项目，**至少占 3 项**。

## 8. How to Stop（结尾）

### 结尾幻灯片 / 段落

❌ **不要**：
- Collaborators 列表（"suggests that nobody knows whether you did anything significant"）
- 致谢页（"squanders real estate"）
- "These are my conclusions"（观众不在乎结论，**在乎你做了什么**）
- "Thank you" / "Thank you for listening"（弱 — 暗示大家出于礼貌才留到这）

✅ **要**：
- **Contributions** — 镜像开头的 problem，列你**实际做了的**
- 留到 Q&A 阶段使用 — 问问题时大家看到的是"你做了啥"

### 收尾词三选一

1. **Joke 笑话** — 收尾时**可以**用，开头不行（"by the time you're done, people have adjusted to your voice parameters"）
2. **Salute the audience 致敬** — "It's been great. I've been stimulated by your questions."
3. **Benediction 祝词 / 格言** — 政治演讲的 "God bless you and God bless America"；技术的可以是一句 call-back 到开头

**经典案例**：Christie vs. Clinton 收尾—— Clinton 忍住没说 thank you，**presses his lips together** 的画面比说 thank you 更强。

## 9. 常见错误对照表

| 错误 | 替代 | 出处 |
|---|---|---|
| 用 joke 开场 | Empowerment Promise | "they usually fall flat" |
| 关键观点只讲 1 次 | Cycling × 3 | "20% will be fogged out" |
| 没区分你和别人的观点 | Fence 一句话 | "his is exponential, mine is linear" |
| 没序号 / 没地标 | 显式 enumeration | "first... second... third..." |
| 抛问题马上接话 | 等 ≥7 秒 | "you won't actually die" |
| 灯暗了 | 全亮 | "see slides through closed eyelids" |
| 板书 = 念字 | 删字、加图、加手势 | "tennis match effect" |
| 用激光笔 | 箭头 + 数字 | "no eye contact, no engagement" |
| 结尾说 Thank you | Joke / Salute / Benediction | "weak move" |
| 结尾 = 致谢页 | Contributions 镜像 | "squanders real estate" |

## 10. 一页便签（贴在你显示器上）

```
起：Promise（不是 joke）+ Passion
承：Cycling × 3 | Fence 对比 | Verbal Punctuation | Question + 7s
收：Contributions 镜像开头 → Joke / Salute / Benediction

工具：teach → 黑板/prop；persuade → slides
幻灯片：少字 / 大字 / 无杂物 / 无激光笔 / ≤1 张密集页
记忆：Winston Star (5S) ≥ 3
```
